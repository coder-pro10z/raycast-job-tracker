## 2. What Recruiters & Hiring Managers Look for in Outreach (Blind / LeetCode / Recruiter Surveys)

Technical recruiters and engineering managers at tier-1 firms review 150+ cold messages a week. Common consensus across platforms (TeamBlind, LeetCode Discuss, LinkedIn Talent Insights):

### The 5 Golden Rules of High-Converting Cold Emails

1. **Keep it under 150 words (Mobile-Scannable)**:
   - 70% of hiring managers read cold outreach on their smartphones between meetings.
   - If an email requires scrolling through 4 dense paragraphs, it gets archived unread.

2. **Personalized Architectural Hook (No Boilerplate)**:
   - **Bad**: *"I am writing to express my interest in any suitable software engineering opening at your esteemed organization..."*
   - **Good**: *"I’ve followed Uber's engineering work on managing concurrent trip state transitions with strict p99 latency SLAs..."*

3. **Concrete, Quantified Proof Points**:
   - Tie technical skills directly to measurable results:
     - *"Architected distributed microservices handling 50k RPS with sub-50ms p99 latency"*
     - *"Optimized frontend bundle size by 40% with code-splitting and Webpack/Vite optimization"*
     - *"Designed idempotent payment webhook handlers guaranteeing zero dropped states"*

4. **Low-Friction Call-to-Action (CTA)**:
   - Never ask: *"Can you interview me this week?"* or *"Please review my attached 4-page resume."*
   - Instead ask: *"Would you be open to a 10–15 minute conversation next week to see if my background matches your team's upcoming roadmap?"*

5. **Never Attach PDFs in the First Cold Email**:
   - PDF/Word attachments trigger aggressive corporate spam filters (Google Workspace and Microsoft Defender).
   - Put clean Markdown links to your **GitHub**, **Portfolio / Live Demos**, and **LinkedIn** directly into your email signature block.
